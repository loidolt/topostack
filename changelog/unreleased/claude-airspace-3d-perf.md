---
type: fix
title: Keep the studio quick with Airspace in 3D on
---
With Airspace in 3D on, changing map detail, labels or line style no longer rebuilds the airspace pieces and rods, so the preview updates in a fraction of a second instead of several. Building the airspace in the first place is two to five times faster, and the 3D stack orbits smoothly again over busy special use airspace.
